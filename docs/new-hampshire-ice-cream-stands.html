Ice cream stands

51 ice cream stands, dairy bars and farm creameries, from the North Woods to the Seacoast. Each is on Visit NH's 2026 Ice Cream Trail, the state tourism office's own passport, checked in fall 2026.

Ask for early accessThe free Granite State Eats app for iPhone and iPad is on its way to the App Store.

Made here, scooped here

Visit NH's 2026 Ice Cream Trail marks the shops owned by a New Hampshire dairy farmer, the ones that make their ice cream on site and the ones that use only New Hampshire milk and cream. Hatchland Farm in North Haverhill and the Sandwich Creamery carry all three marks. Bethlehem's Super Secret Ice Cream, made on site, was a James Beard Outstanding Bakery finalist in 2025 and 2026. We left out the trail's candy shops and chains; seasons vary, so call ahead.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Laconia (3), Keene (2), Nashua (2), Rochester (2), Sandwich (2), Sunapee (2), Bethlehem (1), Portsmouth (1).

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (a dated story, or the state tourism office's own 2026 publication) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score.

On your phone, Granite State Eats orders this list by how far each place is from you, and a tap opens Apple Maps' live card with hours, photos and directions.
