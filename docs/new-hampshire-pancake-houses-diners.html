Pancake houses & diners

8 places for breakfast the New Hampshire way: pancake houses and sugar-house restaurants pouring their own maple syrup, and classic diners. Each was checked in fall 2026 against a 2025 or 2026 source.

Syrup from the sugar house

NH Magazine's 2025 pancake-season guide sends you to Polly's Pancake Parlor in Sugar Hill, an institution for more than 85 years that grinds its own flour and makes its own syrup, to Parker's Maple Barn in Mason, Stuart & John's Sugar House in Westmoreland and Benton's Sugar Shack in Thornton, and to the Intervale Farm and Heritage Farm pancake houses. Tasting Table dates Manchester's Red Arrow Diner to 1922.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Manchester (1), Thornton (1), Sanbornton (1), Henniker (1), Mason (1), Sugar Hill (1), Westmoreland (1), Walpole (1).

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (a dated story, or the state tourism office's own 2026 publication) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score.
